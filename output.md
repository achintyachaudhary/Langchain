
## Gamma Analytics: HR Policy Handbook

Sample document | Effective 1 November 2026 | Owner: Human Resources | Applies to all employees

## 1. Purpose and Scope

- This handbook sets out the people policies that guide how Gamma Analytics employs, supports and develops its team.
- It applies to all full-time, part-time, probationary, contract and intern staff across every office and remote location.
- Policies are meant to be fair, consistent and easy to understand for managers and employees alike.
- Where local law is stricter than a policy, the law prevails.
- Questions can be directed to the HR team at hr@gammaanalytics.example.

## 2. Equal Opportunity and Non-Discrimination

- Gamma Analytics provides equal employment opportunity to all, regardless of gender, age, religion, caste, disability, marital status or background.
- Hiring, pay, promotion and termination decisions are based only on merit, skills and business need.
- Discrimination, bias or unfair treatment by any employee, manager or vendor is not tolerated.
- Reasonable adjustments are made for employees with disabilities.
- Concerns can be raised confidentially with HR without fear of retaliation.

## 3. Recruitment and Selection

- Open roles are advertised internally first, then externally where needed.
- Candidates go through a structured process of screening, technical assessment and interviews.
- Interview panels use consistent criteria so every candidate is evaluated fairly.
- Offers are made in writing and depend on background and reference verification.
- Employees may refer candidates and receive a referral bonus once the hire completes six months.

## 4. Onboarding and Probation

- Every new joiner receives an induction covering company values, tools, policies and team introductions.
- A buddy is assigned for the first 90 days to help the new hire settle in.
- The standard probation period is six months, with check-ins at 30, 90 and 150 days.
- Confirmation is based on performance, conduct and cultural fit.
- Probation may be extended once by up to three months if expectations are not yet met.

## 5. Working Hours and Attendance

- Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour break.
- Employees are expected to mark attendance daily through the HR portal.
- Flexible start times between 8:30 AM and 10:30 AM are allowed with manager approval.
- Repeated late arrivals or unplanned absences are discussed with the reporting manager.
- Overtime must be pre-approved and is compensated through time off or as per applicable law.

## 6. Remote and Hybrid Work

- Eligible roles may work in a hybrid model of three days in office and two days remote.
- Fully remote arrangements need written approval from the manager and HR.
- Remote employees must remain reachable during core hours of 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
- Company laptops and secure VPN access are mandatory when working from home.
- The Company may withdraw remote work privileges if performance or security standards slip.

## 7. Leave Policy

- Employees receive 18 days of paid leave, 8 days of sick leave and 2 days of casual leave per year.
- Unused paid leave of up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next year.
- Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal and approved by the manager.
- Maternity leave of 26 weeks and paternity leave of 10 working days are provided as per law and Company policy.
- Bereavement leave of up to 5 days is granted for the loss of an immediate family member.

## 8. Holidays

- The Company publishes an annual holiday calendar in January with 12 paid public holidays.
- Employees may choose two floating holidays for regional or personal festivals.
- Work on a declared holiday is compensated with a compensatory off.
- Offices remain closed between 25 December and 1 January, and this does not reduce leave balance.
- Holiday lists may vary slightly by office location.

## 9. Compensation and Payroll

- Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month by bank transfer.
- Pay is reviewed against market benchmarks and individual performance once a year.
- Each employee receives a salary slip showing earnings, deductions and statutory contributions.
- Employer contributions such as provident fund and gratuity are provided as per law.
- Salary details are confidential and employees are encouraged not to discuss them in the workplace.

## 10. Performance Management

- Performance is reviewed twice a year through mid-year and annual cycles.
- Each employee sets clear goals with their manager at the start of the cycle.
- Reviews combine self-assessment, manager feedback and peer input.
- Ratings feed into pay revisions, bonuses and promotion decisions.
- Employees who need support are given a structured improvement plan with clear targets and timelines.

## 11. Learning and Development

- Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 30,000 for courses, books and certifications.
- Employees can take up to five paid learning days per year for training or exams.
- Internal workshops, tech talks and mentoring programmes run throughout the year.
- Sponsored certifications may require a service commitment of 12 months.
- Managers discuss growth plans and career paths with their team at least twice a year.

## 12. Code of Conduct

- Employees are expected to act with honesty, integrity and respect in every interaction.
- Professional language and behaviour apply to meetings, chats, emails and social events.
- Company resources must be used only for legitimate business purposes.
- Employees must not misrepresent the Company or make unauthorised public statements on its behalf.
- Any breach of the code may lead to disciplinary action, up to termination.

## 13. Anti-Harassment and POSH

- Gamma Analytics has zero tolerance for sexual harassment, bullying and intimidation.
- An Internal Committee, constituted under the POSH Act, handles complaints of sexual harassment.
- Complaints are investigated promptly, fairly and with strict confidentiality.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited.
- All employees complete mandatory awareness training once every year.

## 14. Data Privacy and Confidentiality

- Employees handle client, company and personal data only for authorised purposes.
- Confidential information must not be shared outside the Company without written approval.
- Client datasets must be stored only in approved, access-controlled systems.
- Personal data of employees is collected and used only as needed for employment and law.
- Confidentiality obligations continue after an employee leaves the Company.

## 15. Information Security and Acceptable Use

- Strong passwords and multi-factor authentication are mandatory on all work accounts.
- Company devices must be kept updated and must not be shared with others.
- Unapproved software, personal cloud storage and external drives must not be used for work data.
- Suspected phishing, data loss or security incidents must be reported to IT within one hour.
- Use of public AI tools with client or confidential data is not allowed without approval.

## 16. Conflict of Interest and Gifts

- Employees must disclose any personal, financial or family interest that could affect business decisions.
- Outside work, freelancing or advisory roles require prior written approval.
- Gifts or hospitality worth more than INR 3,000 from clients or vendors must be declined or reported.
- Cash gifts and favours linked to business decisions are never acceptable.
- Anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws apply to all dealings, in India and abroad.

## 17. Travel and Expense Reimbursement

- Business travel must be approved by the manager before booking.
- Employees fly economy and stay in Company-approved hotels within set city limits.
- Daily meal and local travel allowances are paid as per the travel grade.
- Expense claims with valid receipts must be filed within 15 days of the trip.
- Personal expenses and fines are not reimbursable.

## 18. Health, Safety and Wellbeing

- The Company provides group health insurance for the employee, spouse and dependent children.
- Annual health check-ups and access to a confidential counselling helpline are offered at no cost.
- Offices follow fire safety, first-aid and emergency evacuation procedures with regular drills.
- Managers are encouraged to prevent burnout by keeping workloads and deadlines realistic.
- Smoking, alcohol and illegal substances are not permitted on Company premises.

## 19. Grievance Redressal

- Employees may raise work-related concerns with their manager, HR or the grievance portal.
- Every grievance is acknowledged within two working days.
- HR aims to investigate and resolve most cases within 15 working days.
- Employees can escalate to the Head of HR if they are not satisfied with the outcome.
- Records are kept confidential and used only for resolution and process improvement.

## 20. Disciplinary Action

- Disciplinary steps are applied fairly and in proportion to the seriousness of the issue.
- Typical stages include a verbal warning, written warning, final warning and termination.
- Serious misconduct such as fraud, harassment or data theft may lead to immediate action.
- The employee is given a fair chance to explain their side before a decision is made.
- All actions are documented and reviewed by HR before they are issued.

## 21. Resignation, Notice and Exit

- Employees must submit a written resignation to their manager and HR.
- The notice period is 15 days during probation and 60 days after confirmation.
- Early release may be approved by management, and buy-out of notice is possible with consent.
- Employees must complete a handover, return Company assets and attend an exit interview.
- Full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day.

## 22. Policy Review and Amendments

- HR reviews this handbook at least once a year to keep it current and compliant.
- Changes are approved by senior leadership and communicated by email.
- Updated policies take effect from the date stated in the announcement.
- Employees are responsible for reading and following the latest version.
- This handbook does not form a contract of employment and may be changed at the Company's discretion.
